# Email, Waitlist & Marketing Communications Compliance

Under the NDPA, direct marketing is tightly regulated. Data subjects have a strong right to object, and consent must be real.

## Core Rules

- Every marketing email (including “we launched”, product updates, newsletters) must include a **working, one-click unsubscribe / opt-out** mechanism.
- Include clear identification of the sender (organisation name and a physical or equivalent contact address).
- Consent for marketing must be separate, specific, and freely given. Bundling it with terms of service or pre-ticking boxes is not valid.
- Purchased or scraped lists, or contacts collected without documented consent, are high risk.
- Honour opt-outs immediately and permanently. Do not process that data for marketing again.
- Transactional emails (order confirmations, password resets, service notices) have different rules, but still need clear identification and should not be used as a back-door for marketing.

## Waitlist / Launch Emails
- Collecting emails for a waitlist is processing personal data.
- Tell people what they are signing up for.
- When you later send the “we launched” email, it is marketing. Include unsubscribe and proper identification.
- Keep records of how and when consent was obtained.

## Agent Rules
- Any email template or notification system that is promotional must include:
  - Working unsubscribe link
  - Organisation name + contact details
- Never generate a marketing email flow without an opt-out path.
- When building signup / waitlist forms, capture and store evidence of consent (timestamp, source, what was agreed to).
- Separate transactional and marketing messaging where possible.

## Common Failures
- “We launched” emails with no unsubscribe.
- Pre-ticked “send me updates” boxes.
- Using customer support or order emails as marketing lists without fresh consent.
- No physical or registered address in the footer.
