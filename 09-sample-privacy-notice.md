# Sample Privacy Notice Structure (NDPA-Oriented)

Use this as a starting skeleton. Customise to your actual processing. Do not copy-paste blindly.

---

**Privacy Notice – [Your App / Company Name]**

**Last updated:** [Date]

### 1. Who we are
[Full legal name], registered in Nigeria (or state where relevant).  
Contact for privacy requests: [privacy@email]  
Physical / registered address: [Address]

### 2. What personal data we collect
- Account data: name, email, phone (if collected)
- Usage data: IP address, device/browser info, pages visited
- [Any other categories: payment data, location, etc.]
- We do **not** knowingly collect data from children under 18 without parental consent.

### 3. Purposes and lawful bases
| Purpose                    | Lawful basis                  |
|---------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Create and manage account | Contract / consent            |
| Provide the core service  | Contract                       |
| Send service messages     | Contract / legitimate interest|
| Marketing emails          | Consent (opt-in)              |
| Analytics (if used)       | Consent                       |
| Security & fraud prevention | Legitimate interest         |
| Legal compliance          | Legal obligation              |

### 4. Children’s data
We do not target children under 18. If we become aware that we have collected personal data of a child without verified parental consent, we will delete it. Parents/guardians may contact us to request deletion.

### 5. Sharing and recipients
We share data with:
- Hosting / infrastructure providers
- [Payment processor if any]
- [Analytics provider if any – only after consent]
- Professional advisers and authorities when required by law

### 6. Cross-border transfers
Some of our service providers are located outside Nigeria. Where personal data is transferred outside Nigeria we rely on [adequacy / standard contractual clauses / explicit consent / other NDPA s.41–43 basis]. Details available on request.

### 7. Retention
- Account data: while the account is active + [X] months after closure
- Marketing data: until you unsubscribe or we delete inactive records
- Logs: [period]
We delete or anonymise data when it is no longer needed for the stated purposes.

### 8. Your rights under the NDPA
You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Rectify inaccurate data
- Erase data in certain circumstances
- Restrict or object to processing
- Data portability
- Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent)
- Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)

To exercise these rights, email [privacy@email]. We will respond within the statutory timelines.

### 9. Cookies and tracking
We use strictly necessary cookies to make the service work. Analytics / marketing cookies are only set if you consent. See our Cookie Policy / banner for details and to change your preferences.

### 10. Security
We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

### 11. Changes
We may update this notice. Material changes will be notified via the app or email where appropriate.

### 12. Contact
Questions or requests: [privacy@email]  
NDPC: https://ndpc.gov.ng
